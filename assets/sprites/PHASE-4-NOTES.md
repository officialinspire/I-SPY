# Phase 4 asset notes

The five committed SVG sheets are the production source assets for the I SPY Cold War monochrome sprite system. They were authored from a generated visual reference, then normalized into deterministic 64×64 transparent frames so they can be loaded reliably in Phaser and later used by authored tilemaps.

Internal visual-preview renders used during QA are not runtime dependencies; the canonical source assets are the SVG sheets in this directory.
